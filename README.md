## 👋 About Us

MySigrid is a premium virtual executive assistance and remote staffing company, founded in 2016 and headquartered in Singapore, with our core team based in the Philippines. We help executives and growing businesses reclaim their time through dedicated executive assistants, specialist support, and custom AI agents.

We believe in **augmentation over substitution** — technology should strengthen human judgment, not replace it. That philosophy shapes everything our Developer & IT team builds.

## 🛠️ What We Build

- **The MySigrid Platform** — our web and mobile task management and collaboration platform, where clients delegate work, track progress in real time, and communicate with their assistants in one place.
- **AI Agents & Automation** — custom agents and workflows that take on repetitive tasks so our clients and assistants can focus on higher-value work.
- **Internal Tools & Infrastructure** — the systems, integrations, and IT support that keep a remote-first team secure, connected, and productive.
